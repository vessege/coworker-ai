> mas'uliyatida, Mehnat kodeksiga muvofiq. Bu checklist — koordinatsiya.

# 🇺🇿 Xodim ishdan chiqishida — ofis-menejer checklisti

## Oxirgi ish kunigacha
- [ ] HR dan chiqish sanasi va buyruq holatini tasdiqlash
- [ ] Ishlarni topshirish rejasi (kimga, qachon) tuzilgan
- [ ] Mijoz/hamkorlarga mas'ul o'zgarishi haqida xabar (kerak bo'lsa)

## Jihoz va mulk
- [ ] Kompyuter/telefon/jihozlar qaytarib olindi (holati tekshirildi)
- [ ] Kirish kartasi / kalitlar / propusk topshirildi
- [ ] Korporativ karta/avans hisobotlari yopildi (buxgalteriya bilan)

## Hisoblar va kirishlar
- [ ] Korporativ e-mail va tizim hisoblari o'chirildi/muzlatildi (IT)
- [ ] Umumiy parollar almashtirildi (agar bilgan bo'lsa)
- [ ] Hujjat/fayllarga kirish huquqlari qaytarib olindi

## Yakuniy
- [ ] Ish joyi bo'shatildi va tozalandi
- [ ] Topshirish dalolatnomasi imzolandi (jihozlar ro'yxati bilan)
- [ ] Kontakt ma'lumotlari arxivga qayd etildi

# 🇬🇧 Employee Offboarding — Office Manager Checklist

## Before the last working day
- [ ] Confirm exit date and order status with HR
- [ ] Handover plan (to whom, by when) prepared
- [ ] Clients/partners notified of the new contact (if needed)

## Equipment & property
- [ ] Computer/phone/equipment returned (condition checked)
- [ ] Access card / keys / pass returned
- [ ] Corporate card / expense reports closed (with accounting)

## Accounts & access
- [ ] Corporate e-mail and system accounts disabled (IT)
- [ ] Shared passwords rotated (if known to the leaver)
- [ ] Document/file permissions revoked

## Final
- [ ] Workspace cleared
- [ ] Handover act signed (with equipment list)
- [ ] Contact details archived
