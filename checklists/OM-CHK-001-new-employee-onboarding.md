# 🇺🇿 Yangi xodimni ishga qabul qilish — ofis-menejer checklisti

> Eslatma: bu checklist ofis-menejerning **koordinatsiya** vazifalarini qamraydi.
> Mehnat shartnomasi, buyruq va huquqiy hujjatlar HR/rahbar mas'uliyatida va
> O'zbekiston Mehnat kodeksiga muvofiq rasmiylashtiriladi.

## Ishga chiqishdan oldin
- [ ] Ishga qabul buyrug'i tayyorligini HR bilan tasdiqlash
- [ ] Ish joyi (stol, stul) tayyorlash
- [ ] Kompyuter/telefon va zarur jihozlar
- [ ] Korporativ e-mail va tizim(lar)ga hisob (login) ochish so'rovi (IT)
- [ ] Kirish kartasi / kalit / propusk
- [ ] Kanstovarlar to'plami
- [ ] Birinchi kun jadvali va mas'ul mentor tayinlash

## Birinchi kun
- [ ] Kutib olish, ofis bo'ylab tanishtirish (chiqish, xavfsizlik, oshxona)
- [ ] Jamoaga tanishtirish
- [ ] Ish joyi va jihozlarni topshirish
- [ ] Hisoblar va parollarni faollashtirish (IT bilan)
- [ ] Ichki qoidalar (ish vaqti, aloqa kanallari) bilan tanishtirish
- [ ] Zaruriy hujjatlarni imzolash uchun HRga yo'naltirish

## Birinchi hafta
- [ ] Kerakli tizimlarga kirish ishlayotganini tekshirish
- [ ] Zarur reglament/SOP hujjatlariga havola berish
- [ ] Mentor bilan kunlik qisqa uchrashuvlar
- [ ] Savollar uchun ochiq kanal

## Birinchi 30 kun
- [ ] Sinov muddati maqsadlarini rahbar bilan aniqlashtirish
- [ ] Fikr-mulohaza (feedback) yig'ish
- [ ] Ochiq qolgan kirish/jihoz masalalarini yopish

## Chiqib ketishda (offboarding — bog'liq)
- [ ] Jihozlarni qaytarib olish, hisoblarni bloklash — alohida checklist (OM-CHK-002)

# 🇬🇧 New Employee Onboarding — Office Manager Checklist

> Note: this checklist covers the office manager's **coordination** duties.
> The employment contract, hiring order, and legal documents are the
> responsibility of HR/management and are executed per the Labour Code of Uzbekistan.

## Before start date
- [ ] Confirm the hiring order is ready with HR
- [ ] Prepare the workspace (desk, chair)
- [ ] Computer/phone and required equipment
- [ ] Request corporate e-mail and system account(s) (IT)
- [ ] Access card / key / pass
- [ ] Stationery set
- [ ] First-day schedule and assigned mentor

## First day
- [ ] Welcome and office tour (exits, safety, kitchen)
- [ ] Team introductions
- [ ] Hand over workspace and equipment
- [ ] Activate accounts and passwords (with IT)
- [ ] Introduce internal rules (working hours, communication channels)
- [ ] Direct to HR to sign required documents

## First week
- [ ] Verify access to required systems works
- [ ] Share relevant regulations/SOP documents
- [ ] Short daily check-ins with the mentor
- [ ] Open channel for questions

## First 30 days
- [ ] Clarify probation goals with the manager
- [ ] Collect feedback
- [ ] Close any outstanding access/equipment items

## Offboarding (related)
- [ ] Reclaim equipment, disable accounts — separate checklist (OM-CHK-002)
