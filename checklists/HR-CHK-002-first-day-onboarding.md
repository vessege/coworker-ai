# 🇺🇿 Yangi xodimning birinchi kuni — checklist

## Kelishidan oldin
- [ ] Ish joyi va kerakli jihozlar tayyor
- [ ] Elektron pochta / tizim kirishlari yaratilgan
- [ ] Jamoaga xabar berilgan (kim, qaysi lavozim, qaysi bo'lim)

## Birinchi kun
- [ ] Ichki mehnat tartib-qoidalari bilan tanishtirilgan
- [ ] Mehnat muhofazasi yo'riqnomasi o'tkazilgan va imzo qo'ydirilgan
- [ ] Bevosita rahbar va jamoa bilan tanishtirilgan
- [ ] Lavozim vazifalari va birinchi haftalik topshiriqlar tushuntirilgan
- [ ] Kerakli tizim/hisob ma'lumotlari (login, parol) topshirilgan

## Birinchi hafta oxirida
- [ ] Qisqa fikr-mulohaza (feedback) suhbati o'tkazilgan
- [ ] Sinov muddati bo'yicha kutilayotgan natijalar aniq yozilgan

# 🇬🇧 New Employee First Day — Checklist

## Before arrival
- [ ] Workspace and equipment ready
- [ ] Email / system access accounts created
- [ ] Team notified (who, position, department)

## First day
- [ ] Introduced to internal labour regulations
- [ ] Occupational safety briefing done and acknowledged by signature
- [ ] Introduced to direct manager and team
- [ ] Role responsibilities and first-week tasks explained
- [ ] System credentials (login, password) handed over

## End of first week
- [ ] Short feedback conversation held
- [ ] Expected probation-period outcomes documented
