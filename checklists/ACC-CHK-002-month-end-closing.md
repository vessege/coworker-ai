# 🇺🇿 Oy oxirini yopish — checklist

## Hujjatlar
- [ ] Barcha kiruvchi/chiquvchi schyot-fakturalar ro'yxatga olingan
- [ ] Bajarilgan ishlar dalolatnomalari imzolangan va qayd etilgan
- [ ] Kassa/bank hujjatlari to'liq kiritilgan

## Solishtirish
- [ ] Bank ko'chirmasi buxgalteriya yozuvlari bilan solishtirilgan
- [ ] Kontragentlar bilan o'zaro hisob-kitob solishtirilgan (kerak bo'lsa)
- [ ] Ombor qoldiqlari hisobot ma'lumotlariga mos

## Hisobotga tayyorgarlik
- [ ] Oy davomidagi barcha operatsiyalar provodka qilingan
- [ ] Keyingi oy/chorak hisoboti muddati ACC-KA-001 taqvimida tekshirilgan
- [ ] Nomuvofiqliklar (agar bo'lsa) izohlangan va tuzatilgan

# 🇬🇧 Month-End Closing — Checklist

## Documents
- [ ] All incoming/outgoing invoices registered
- [ ] Completed-work acts signed and recorded
- [ ] Cash/bank documents fully entered

## Reconciliation
- [ ] Bank statement reconciled against accounting entries
- [ ] Counterparty balances reconciled (where applicable)
- [ ] Inventory balances match reporting data

## Reporting readiness
- [ ] All transactions for the month posted
- [ ] Next filing deadline checked against the ACC-KA-001 calendar
- [ ] Any discrepancies noted and corrected
