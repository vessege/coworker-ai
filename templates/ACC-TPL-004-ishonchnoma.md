# 🇺🇿 TMB olish uchun ishonchnoma — shablon

```
[TASHKILOT NOMI], STIR [___]
Manzil: [___], h/r [___], bank [___], MFO [___]

                    ISHONCHNOMA № [raqam]

Berilgan sana: [KK.OO.YYYY]     Amal qilish muddati: [KK.OO.YYYY] gacha

Ushbu ishonchnoma [lavozim] [F.I.Sh.]ga (pasport: [seriya, raqam],
berilgan: [kim, qachon]) berildi.

U [yetkazib beruvchi nomi, STIR]dan quyidagi tovar-moddiy boyliklarni
olishga vakil qilinadi:

| № | Nomi | O'lchov birligi | Miqdori |
|---|------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | [...] | [...] | [...] |

Asos: [shartnoma/hisob № va sana].

Ishonchnoma bo'yicha vakil imzosi: ____________ (tasdiqlaymiz)

Rahbar:          ____________ [F.I.Sh.]
Bosh buxgalter:  ____________ [F.I.Sh.]      [M.O'.]
```

## To'ldirish qoidalari
- Muddati aniq ko'rsatiladi (odatda qisqa — bitta olish uchun).
- Vakilning imzo namunasi ishonchnomada tasdiqlanadi.
- Rahbar **va** bosh buxgalter imzolaydi, muhr bosiladi.
- Ishonchnomalar alohida jurnalda ro'yxatga olinadi.

# 🇬🇧 Power of Attorney for Receiving Goods — template

```
[ORGANIZATION NAME], tax ID [___]
Address: [___], account [___], bank [___], MFO [___]

                POWER OF ATTORNEY No. [number]

Issue date: [DD.MM.YYYY]     Valid until: [DD.MM.YYYY]

Issued to [position] [Full name] (passport: [series, number],
issued by: [authority, date]).

Authorized to receive from [supplier name, tax ID] the following goods:

| # | Item | Unit | Qty |
|---|------|------|-----|
| 1 | [...] | [...] | [...] |

Basis: [contract/invoice No. and date].

Specimen signature of the attorney: ____________ (certified)

Head:              ____________ [Full name]
Chief accountant:  ____________ [Full name]      [Seal]
```

## Fill-in rules
- State the validity period explicitly (usually short — one pickup).
- The attorney's specimen signature is certified on the document.
- Signed by the head **and** the chief accountant; sealed.
- Register powers of attorney in a dedicated journal.
