> ℹ️ Bajarilgan ishlar/xizmatlar dalolatnomasi (akt) — ish/xizmat qabul qilinganini

# 🇺🇿 Bajarilgan ishlar (xizmatlar) dalolatnomasi — shablon

```
BAJARILGAN ISHLAR (XIZMATLAR) DALOLATNOMASI № [raqam]

[shahar]                                              [KK.OO.YYYY]

Shartnoma: № [___], [sana]

BUYURTMACHI: [tashkilot nomi], STIR [___], manzil [___]
IJROCHI:     [tashkilot nomi], STIR [___], manzil [___]

Quyidagi ishlar/xizmatlar bajarildi va qabul qilindi:

| № | Ish/xizmat nomi | O'lchov birligi | Miqdor | Narx (QQSsiz) | Summa (QQSsiz) | QQS | Jami |
|---|-----------------|-----------------|--------|---------------|----------------|-----|------|
| 1 | [...]           | [...]           | [...]  | [...]         | [...]          | [...] | [...] |
|   | JAMI            |                 |        |               | [∑]            | [∑] | [∑]  |

Jami summa (QQS bilan): [raqam] so'm ([summa yozuv bilan]).

Tomonlar ishlar/xizmatlar to'liq va sifatli bajarilganini, o'zaro da'volar
yo'qligini tasdiqlaydi.

BUYURTMACHI:                          IJROCHI:
[Lavozim] ___________ [F.I.Sh.]       [Lavozim] ___________ [F.I.Sh.]
        (imzo)  [M.O'.]                       (imzo)  [M.O'.]
```

## To'ldirish qoidalari
- Akt raqami va sanasi ijroga qabul kuniga qo'yiladi.
- Summalar hisobvaraq-faktura bilan mos bo'lishi kerak.
- Ikki tomon imzosi va (bo'lsa) muhr shart.

# 🇬🇧 Act of Completed Works/Services — template

```
ACT OF COMPLETED WORKS (SERVICES) No. [number]

[city]                                                [DD.MM.YYYY]

Contract: No. [___], dated [date]

CUSTOMER:   [organization name], tax ID [___], address [___]
CONTRACTOR: [organization name], tax ID [___], address [___]

The following works/services were completed and accepted:

| # | Work/service | Unit | Qty | Price (excl. VAT) | Amount (excl. VAT) | VAT | Total |
|---|--------------|------|-----|-------------------|--------------------|-----|-------|
| 1 | [...]        | [...]| [...]| [...]            | [...]              | [...] | [...] |
|   | TOTAL        |      |     |                   | [∑]                | [∑] | [∑]   |

Total amount (incl. VAT): [number] UZS ([amount in words]).

The parties confirm the works/services were completed fully and with proper
quality, and that there are no mutual claims.

CUSTOMER:                             CONTRACTOR:
[Position] ___________ [Full name]    [Position] ___________ [Full name]
         (signature) [Seal]                    (signature) [Seal]
```

## Fill-in rules
- The act number and date are set on the day of acceptance.
- Amounts must match the invoice (schyot-faktura).
- Signatures of both parties and a seal (if used) are required.
