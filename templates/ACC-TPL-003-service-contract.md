# 🇺🇿 Xizmat ko'rsatish shartnomasi — shablon

```
XIZMAT KO'RSATISH SHARTNOMASI № [raqam]

[shahar]                                              [KK.OO.YYYY]

BUYURTMACHI: [nom], STIR [___], manzil [___], [rahbar F.I.Sh.] nomidan
IJROCHI:     [nom], STIR [___], manzil [___], [rahbar F.I.Sh.] nomidan

1. SHARTNOMA PREDMETI
   Ijrochi quyidagi xizmatlarni ko'rsatadi: [xizmat tavsifi].

2. SHARTNOMA QIYMATI VA TO'LOV TARTIBI
   Umumiy qiymat: [summa] so'm (QQS [bilan/siz]).
   To'lov tartibi: [oldindan/bosqichma-bosqich/ish tugagach], [muddat].

3. TOMONLAR HUQUQ VA MAJBURIYATLARI
   [asosiy majburiyatlar]

4. IJRO MUDDATLARI
   Boshlanishi: [sana]. Tugashi: [sana].

5. ISHNI QABUL QILISH
   Xizmat bajarilgan ishlar dalolatnomasi (akt) bilan qabul qilinadi.

6. JAVOBGARLIK
   [penya, jarima shartlari]

7. NIZOLARNI HAL ETISH
   Nizolar muzokara yo'li bilan, kelishilmasa — sud tartibida.

8. AMAL QILISH MUDDATI
   [sana] dan [sana] gacha.

9. TOMONLAR REKVIZITLARI VA IMZOLARI
   BUYURTMACHI:                       IJROCHI:
   [rekvizitlar]                      [rekvizitlar]
   ___________ [F.I.Sh.] [M.O'.]      ___________ [F.I.Sh.] [M.O'.]
```

## To'ldirish qoidalari
- STIR, manzil, bank rekvizitlari to'liq ko'rsatilsin.
- QQS bilan/siz ekanini aniq yozing (rejimi ga bog'liq).
- Qabul akti va hisobvaraq-faktura shartnomaga bog'lansin.

# 🇬🇧 Service Contract — template

```
SERVICE CONTRACT No. [number]

[city]                                                [DD.MM.YYYY]

CUSTOMER:   [name], tax ID [___], address [___], represented by [full name]
CONTRACTOR: [name], tax ID [___], address [___], represented by [full name]

1. SUBJECT
   The Contractor provides the following services: [description].

2. PRICE AND PAYMENT
   Total: [amount] UZS (VAT [incl./excl.]).
   Payment: [advance/staged/on completion], [term].

3. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS
   [key obligations]

4. TIMELINE
   Start: [date]. End: [date].

5. ACCEPTANCE
   Services are accepted via an act of completed works.

6. LIABILITY
   [penalty terms]

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   By negotiation; failing that, in court.

8. TERM
   From [date] to [date].

9. DETAILS AND SIGNATURES
   CUSTOMER:                          CONTRACTOR:
   [details]                          [details]
   ___________ [name] [seal]          ___________ [name] [seal]
```

## Fill-in rules
- Provide full tax ID, address, and bank details.
- State clearly whether prices include VAT (depends on regime).
- Link the acceptance act and invoice to this contract.
