# 🇺🇿 Soliq muddatlari — savol-javob

**S: QQS hisobotini qachon topshiraman?**
J: Odatda har oyning **20-sanasigacha** (o'tgan oy uchun). Manba: buxgalter.uz.

**S: Foyda solig'i hisobotini qachon topshiraman?**
J: QQS bilan birga, oyning **20-sanasigacha**.

**S: Aylanma (aylanmadan olinadigan) soliqni qachon topshiraman?**
J: **Har chorak yakuni bo'yicha**, shaxsiy kabinet orqali elektron. Bazaviy stavka 4%.

**S: Ijtimoiy soliq/badallarni qachon to'layman?**
J: Reyestr oyning **15-sanasigacha** topshiriladi va to'lanadi.

**S: Mol-mulk va yer solig'i bo'nagini qachon to'layman?**
J: Oyning **10-sanasigacha**.

**S: Muddat dam olish kuniga to'g'ri kelsa-chi?**
J: Keyingi ish kuniga suriladi.

**S: Kechiktirsam nima bo'ladi?**
J: To'lov kechikkani uchun **penya**, hisobot kechikkani uchun **jarima** (miqdorlar Soliq kodeksida). Aniq holatni buxgalteringiz bilan tekshiring.

# 🇬🇧 Tax Deadlines — FAQ

**Q: When do I file the VAT report?**
A: Usually by the **20th** of each month (for the previous month). Source: buxgalter.uz.

**Q: When do I file the profit tax report?**
A: Together with VAT, by the **20th**.

**Q: When do I file turnover tax?**
A: **After each quarter-end**, electronically via the personal cabinet. Base rate 4%.

**Q: When do I pay social tax/contributions?**
A: The register is filed and paid by the **15th**.

**Q: When do I pay property and land tax advances?**
A: By the **10th**.

**Q: What if the deadline falls on a non-working day?**
A: It moves to the next working day.

**Q: What happens if I'm late?**
A: **Penalty interest (penya)** for late payment, a **fine** for late filing (amounts per the Tax Code). Verify your specific case with your accountant.
