# 🇺🇿 Mehnat huquqi — savol-javob

**S: Sinov muddati qancha bo'lishi mumkin?**
J: Ko'pi bilan **3 oy**; rahbar, o'rinbosar va bosh buxgalter uchun **6 oygacha**. Shartnomada yozilmagan bo'lsa — sinov yo'q hisoblanadi.

**S: Yillik ta'til necha kun?**
J: Kamida **21 kalendar kun** (MK 217-modda). Har 5 yillik staj uchun +2 kun (maks +8, 220-modda).

**S: Mehnat shartnomasi og'zaki bo'lishi mumkinmi?**
J: Yo'q. Faqat **yozma**, kamida 2 nusxada, ish boshlashdan oldin.

**S: Sinov davrida kasal bo'lsam, muddat uzayadimi?**
J: Ha — vaqtincha mehnatga layoqatsizlik kunlari sinov muddatiga **kirmaydi**.

**S: Buyruqsiz ishga olish mumkinmi?**
J: Shartnoma asosiy hujjat, lekin amaliyotda buyruq ham chiqariladi va xodim imzo bilan tanishadi.

**S: Ta'til kalendar kunda hisoblanadimi?**
J: Ha, yangi kodeksdan beri **kalendar kunlarda** (avval ish kunida edi).

# 🇬🇧 Labour Law — FAQ

**Q: How long can probation be?**
A: Max **3 months**; up to **6 months** for heads, deputies, chief accountants. Not in the contract = no probation.

**Q: How many days of annual leave?**
A: Minimum **21 calendar days** (art. 217). +2 days per 5 years of service (max +8, art. 220).

**Q: Can an employment contract be verbal?**
A: No. **Written** only, at least 2 copies, before work starts.

**Q: Does sickness extend probation?**
A: Yes — days of temporary incapacity **don't count** toward probation.

**Q: Is a hiring order required?**
A: The contract is primary, but in practice an order is issued and acknowledged by signature.

**Q: Is leave counted in calendar days?**
A: Yes, since the new Code — **calendar days** (previously working days).
