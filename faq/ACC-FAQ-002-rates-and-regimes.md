# 🇺🇿 Soliq stavkalari va rejimlari — savol-javob

**S: QQS stavkasi qancha?** J: **12%** (umumiy rejim).
**S: Foyda solig'i stavkasi qancha?** J: bazaviy **15%** (ayrim faoliyatlarda farqli).
**S: Aylanma soliq stavkasi qancha?** J: yuridik shaxs uchun bazaviy **4%**; YaTT (≤1 mlrd) uchun **1%**.
**S: YaTT qancha ijtimoiy soliq to'laydi?** J: har oy **1 BHM**.
**S: Qachon QQS to'lovchisi bo'laman?** J: aylanmangiz chegaradan oshsa yoki ixtiyoriy ro'yxatdan o'tsangiz — umumiy rejimga o'tasiz.
**S: 2026-yilda stavkalar oshdimi?** J: asosiy soliqlar bo'yicha stavkalar **o'zgarmaydi** (kun.uz).
**S: Aylanma soliq qachon topshiriladi?** J: har chorak yakuni bo'yicha, shaxsiy kabinet orqali.

# 🇬🇧 Tax Rates & Regimes — FAQ

**Q: What is the VAT rate?** A: **12%** (general regime).
**Q: What is the profit tax rate?** A: base **15%** (varies by activity).
**Q: What is the turnover tax rate?** A: base **4%** for legal entities; **1%** for sole proprietors (≤1 bln).
**Q: How much social tax does a sole proprietor pay?** A: **1 base amount** per month.
**Q: When do I become a VAT payer?** A: when turnover exceeds the threshold or you register voluntarily — you move to the general regime.
**Q: Did rates rise in 2026?** A: main tax rates are **unchanged** (kun.uz).
**Q: When is turnover tax filed?** A: after each quarter-end, via the personal cabinet.
