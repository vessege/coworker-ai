# 🇺🇿 Ish yuritish — savol-javob

**S: Hujjat raqami qanday beriladi?**
J: Jurnaldan **ketma-ket va noyob**; tavsiya format: `[ish indeksi]/[tartib raqam]` (masalan `01-12/45`). Raqam qayta ishlatilmaydi.

**S: Kiruvchi xatni avval kimga beraman — rahbargami?**
J: Avval **ro'yxatga olasiz**, keyin rahbarga. Ro'yxatsiz hujjat ijrochiga berilmaydi (OM-BR-001).

**S: "Shaxsan" belgili konvertni ochsam bo'ladimi?**
J: Yo'q — ochmasdan adresatning o'ziga uzatiladi.

**S: Xatni imzosiz jo'natsam bo'ladimi?**
J: Yo'q. Faqat vakolatli shaxs imzolaydi; imzosiz xat yaroqsiz (OM-SOP-002).

**S: Muhr qayerga bosiladi?**
J: "M.O'." belgilangan joyga, imzo ustiga tushmasin. Muhr hujjat haqiqiyligini tasdiqlaydi.

**S: Ijro muddati ko'rsatilmagan bo'lsa-chi?**
J: Ichki reglament standarti qo'llanadi (odatda 10 ish kuni), rezolyutsiyada boshqacha bo'lmasa.

**S: Hujjatlar qancha saqlanadi?**
J: Nomenklaturada har ish uchun saqlash muddati belgilanadi; muddati o'tgach arxivga o'tkaziladi.

# 🇬🇧 Office Management — FAQ

**Q: How are document numbers assigned?**
A: **Sequential and unique** from the register; recommended format `[case index]/[sequence]` (e.g. `01-12/45`). Numbers are never reused.

**Q: Do I give an incoming letter to the manager first?**
A: **Register first**, then to the manager. Unregistered documents are never routed (OM-BR-001).

**Q: May I open an envelope marked "personal"?**
A: No — forward it unopened to the addressee.

**Q: Can a letter go out unsigned?**
A: No. Only an authorized person signs; unsigned letters are void (OM-SOP-002).

**Q: Where does the seal go?**
A: On the "M.O'." mark, not over the signature. The seal certifies authenticity.

**Q: What if no execution deadline is stated?**
A: The internal standard applies (commonly 10 working days) unless the resolution says otherwise.

**Q: How long are documents kept?**
A: The nomenclature sets a retention period per case; afterwards files go to the archive.
