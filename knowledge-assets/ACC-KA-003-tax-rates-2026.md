# 🇺🇿 Asosiy soliq stavkalari (2026)

| Soliq | Stavka | Izoh |
|-------|--------|------|
| **QQS (qo'shilgan qiymat solig'i)** | **12%** | Umumiy rejim to'lovchilari |
| **Foyda solig'i** | **15%** (bazaviy) | Ayrim faoliyat turlarida past/yuqori |
| **Aylanmadan olinadigan soliq (yuridik shaxs)** | **4%** (bazaviy) | Hudud/faoliyat/eksportga qarab pasayishi mumkin |
| **Aylanma soliq (YaTT ≤ 1 mlrd)** | **1%** | Yillik aylanma 1 mlrd so'mgacha |
| **Ijtimoiy soliq (YaTT)** | **oyiga 1 BHM** | Har oy to'lanadi |

- 2026-yilda asosiy soliqlar bo'yicha stavkalar o'zgarmaydi (kun.uz, 18.12.2025).
- BHM (bazaviy hisoblash miqdori) davlat tomonidan belgilanadi — joriy qiymatni soliq.uz/lex.uz da tekshiring.

# 🇬🇧 Key Tax Rates (2026)

| Tax | Rate | Note |
|-----|------|------|
| **VAT** | **12%** | General-regime payers |
| **Profit tax** | **15%** (base) | Lower/higher for certain activities |
| **Turnover tax (legal entity)** | **4%** (base) | May be reduced by region/activity/export |
| **Turnover tax (sole proprietor ≤ 1 bln)** | **1%** | Annual turnover up to 1 bln UZS |
| **Social tax (sole proprietor)** | **1 base calc amount / month** | Paid monthly |

- Main tax rates are unchanged for 2026 (kun.uz, 18.12.2025).
- The base calculation amount (BHM) is set by the state — check the current value on soliq.uz/lex.uz.
