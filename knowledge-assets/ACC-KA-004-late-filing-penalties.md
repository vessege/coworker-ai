# 🇺🇿 Kechikish jarimalari

## Ikki xil oqibat
- **Penya** — soliqni **o'z vaqtida to'lamaslik** uchun (har kun uchun hisoblanadi).
- **Jarima** — **hisobotni o'z vaqtida topshirmaslik** yoki soliqni kam ko'rsatish uchun.

## Kichik biznes uchun 2026 yengilliklari (manba: buxgalter.uz)
- Hisobotni kechiktirganlik uchun **ma'muriy jarima** kichik tadbirkorlik mansabdor shaxslariga **10 BHM dan 3 BHM ga** (~1,2 mln so'm) kamaytirilmoqda; fuqarolar uchun 3 dan 1 BHM ga (~412 ming so'm).
- **Moliyaviy jarima** ham kichik biznes uchun 10 BHM dan 3 BHM ga kamaytirilmoqda.

## "Yengillik" sharti (ma'muriy ish qo'zg'atilmaydi)
Agar soliq to'lovchi oxirgi **3 oy** hisobotlarni o'z vaqtida topshirgan bo'lsa **va** navbatdagi hisobot **5 ish kunidan** ortiq kechikmagan bo'lsa — ma'muriy huquqbuzarlik ishi boshlanmaydi.

## Amaliy tavsiya
- Muddatdan oldin topshiring; 5 ish kunidan oshirmang.
- Penyadan qochish uchun to'lovni muddatida amalga oshiring.
- Aniq miqdor va holatni buxgalter/soliq.uz bilan tasdiqlang.

# 🇬🇧 Late-Filing Penalties

## Two types of consequence
- **Penalty interest (penya)** — for **late payment** of tax (accrued per day).
- **Fine (jarima)** — for **late filing** of a report or under-declaring tax.

## 2026 relief for small business (source: buxgalter.uz)
- The **administrative fine** for late filing is being reduced for small-business officials from **10 to 3 base amounts** (~1.2 mln UZS); for citizens from 3 to 1 (~412k UZS).
- The **financial penalty** for small business is likewise being reduced from 10 to 3 base amounts.

## "Relief" condition (no administrative case opened)
If the taxpayer filed on time for the last **3 months** **and** the next report is delayed no more than **5 working days**, no administrative-offence case is opened.

## Practical advice
- File before the deadline; never exceed 5 working days late.
- Pay on time to avoid penalty interest.
- Confirm exact amounts and your situation with an accountant / soliq.uz.
