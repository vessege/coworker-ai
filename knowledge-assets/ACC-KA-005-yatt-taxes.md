# 🇺🇿 YaTT soliqlari va majburiyatlari (2026)

## Asosiy to'lovlar
| To'lov | Miqdor | Qachon |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Aylanma soliq** (aylanma ≤ 1 mlrd so'm) | **1%** | Choraklik, shaxsiy kabinet orqali |
| **Ijtimoiy soliq** | **oyiga 1 BHM** | Har oy |
| **Yillik deklaratsiya** | — | Keyingi yil **1-martgacha** |

## Chegaralar va o'tishlar
- Yillik aylanma **1 mlrd so'mdan oshsa** → 1% huquqi yo'qoladi, keyingi oyning 1-kunidan **umumiy rejimga** (QQS 12% + foyda solig'i) o'tiladi.
- Yillik tushumi 100 mln so'mdan oshgan tadbirkor **ixtiyoriy qat'iy summa** variantini tanlab, hisobot topshirishdan ozod bo'lishi mumkin (azma.uz).

## Amaliy eslatmalar
- Aylanmani oylik kuzatib boring — chegaraga yaqinlashganda buxgalter bilan rejim o'tishini oldindan rejalashtiring.
- Ijtimoiy soliq to'lovi pensiya stajiga ta'sir qiladi — o'tkazib yubormang.
- Xodim yollasangiz — ish haqidan soliq/badallar bo'yicha alohida majburiyatlar boshlanadi.

# 🇬🇧 Sole Proprietor (YaTT) Taxes (2026)

## Core payments
| Payment | Amount | When |
|---------|--------|------|
| **Turnover tax** (turnover ≤ 1 bln UZS) | **1%** | Quarterly, via personal cabinet |
| **Social tax** | **1 BHM/month** | Monthly |
| **Annual declaration** | — | By **March 1** of the next year |

## Thresholds and transitions
- If annual turnover **exceeds 1 bln UZS** → the 1% right is lost; from the 1st of the next month the **general regime** applies (VAT 12% + profit tax).
- Entrepreneurs with revenue over 100 mln UZS may opt into a **fixed-sum** scheme and skip reporting (azma.uz).

## Practical notes
- Track turnover monthly; plan the regime transition before hitting the threshold.
- Social tax payments count toward pension record — don't skip them.
- Hiring an employee triggers separate payroll tax/contribution duties.
