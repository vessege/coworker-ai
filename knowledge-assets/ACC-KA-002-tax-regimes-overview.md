# 🇺🇿 KO'B soliq rejimlari

## Ikki asosiy rejim
| Rejim | Kim | Asosiy soliq |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| **Soddalashtirilgan (aylanma soliq)** | Yillik aylanmasi chegaradan past KO'B | Aylanmadan olinadigan soliq (bazaviy 4%) |
| **Umumiy rejim** | Aylanmasi chegaradan yuqori yoki ixtiyoriy | QQS (12%) + foyda solig'i (15%) |

## Chegaralar (2026 — tasdiqlang)
- **YaTT / o'zini-o'zi band qilgan:** yillik aylanma **1 mlrd so'm**gacha → aylanma soliq **1%**.
- **Yuridik shaxs:** aylanma soliq bazaviy stavkasi 4%. Umumiy rejimga/QQSga o'tish chegarasi 1-iyun 2026 dan **5 mlrd so'm**ga oshirilgani xabar qilingan (manbalarda ziddiyat — soliq.uz da tekshiring).
- Chegara oshsa → keyingi oyning 1-kunidan umumiy rejimga o'tiladi (QQS + foyda solig'i to'lovchisi bo'linadi).

## Ba'zi imtiyozlar (aylanma soliqdan ozod, misollar)
Ta'lim/maktabgacha ta'lim tashkilotlari, keksa/bemor parvarishi, nogironligi bo'lganlar uchun mahsulot ishlab chiqaruvchilar va boshqalar (to'liq ro'yxatni soliq.uz da tekshiring).

## Qanday tanlanadi
1. Yillik aylanmani baholang → chegaradan past bo'lsa soddalashtirilgan mumkin.
2. Aksiz mahsuloti / foydali qazilma / davlat ulushi bo'lsa — chegara qo'llanmaydi.
3. Mijozlaringiz QQS talab qilsa — umumiy rejim afzalroq bo'lishi mumkin.

# 🇬🇧 SME Tax Regimes

## Two main regimes
| Regime | Who | Main tax |
|--------|-----|----------|
| **Simplified (turnover tax)** | SMEs with annual turnover below the threshold | Turnover tax (base 4%) |
| **General regime** | Turnover above threshold, or voluntary | VAT (12%) + profit tax (15%) |

## Thresholds (2026 — verify)
- **Sole proprietor / self-employed:** annual turnover up to **1 bln UZS** → turnover tax **1%**.
- **Legal entity:** turnover tax base rate 4%. The transition threshold to the general/VAT regime was reportedly raised to **5 bln UZS** from June 1, 2026 (sources conflict — verify on soliq.uz).
- If the threshold is exceeded → switch to the general regime from the 1st of the next month (become a VAT + profit tax payer).

## Some exemptions (from turnover tax, examples)
Education/pre-school organizations, elderly/patient care, manufacturers employing people with disabilities, and others (verify the full list on soliq.uz).

## How to choose
1. Estimate annual turnover → below threshold, simplified is possible.
2. Excise goods / minerals / state share → threshold does not apply.
3. If your clients require VAT invoices → the general regime may be preferable.
