# 🇺🇿 Soliq hisobot va to'lov taqvimi (yuridik shaxslar)

## Takrorlanuvchi oylik muddatlar (umumiy rejim)
| Sana | Nima qilinadi |
|------|---------------|
| **10-sana** | Aksiz solig'i hisoboti taqdim etiladi; mol-mulk va yer solig'i bo'yicha bo'nak to'lovlari |
| **15-sana** | Ijtimoiy soliq/majburiy badallar reyestri (ShJShB) taqdim etiladi va to'lanadi |
| **20-sana** | **QQS hisoboti** va **foyda solig'i** hisoblari topshiriladi; norezidentlar daromadi solig'i hisoboti |

## Takrorlanuvchi choraklik muddatlar
| Muddat | Nima qilinadi |
|--------|---------------|
| **Har chorak yakuni bo'yicha** | **Aylanmadan olinadigan soliq** hisoboti shaxsiy kabinet orqali elektron taqdim etiladi (bazaviy stavka 4%) |

## Yillik
| Muddat | Nima qilinadi |
|--------|---------------|
| **Keyingi yil 1-mart** | Yillik deklaratsiya (masalan YaTT jismoniy shaxs daromadlari) topshiriladi |

## Muhim izohlar
- **Kechikish oqibati:** o'z vaqtida to'lamaslik uchun **penya**, hisobotni kechiktirish uchun **jarima** solinadi (miqdorlar Soliq kodeksida).
- **Chegara (2026, tekshirilishi kerak):** manbalarda ziddiyat bor — aylanma soliq uchun yillik daromad **1 mlrd so'm** chegarasi ko'rsatilgan (azma.uz), biroq 1-iyun 2026 islohoti umumiy rejim/QQSga o'tish chegarasini **5 mlrd so'm**ga oshirgani xabar qilingan (spot.uz, gazeta.uz). **Aniq amaldagi chegarani soliq.uz da tasdiqlang.**
- **Sana suriladi:** muddat dam olish kuniga to'g'ri kelsa — keyingi ish kuniga o'tadi.

# 🇬🇧 Tax Reporting & Payment Calendar (Legal Entities)

## Recurring monthly deadlines (general regime)
| Date | Action |
|------|--------|
| **10th** | Excise tax report filed; advance payments for property and land tax |
| **15th** | Social tax / mandatory contribution register (payroll) filed and paid |
| **20th** | **VAT report** and **profit tax** calculations filed; non-resident income tax report |

## Recurring quarterly deadlines
| Deadline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **After each quarter-end** | **Turnover tax** report filed electronically via the personal cabinet (base rate 4%) |

## Annual
| Deadline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **March 1 of the following year** | Annual declaration (e.g. sole-proprietor personal income) |

## Important notes
- **Late consequences:** late payment incurs **penalty interest (penya)**; late filing incurs a **fine** (amounts per the Tax Code).
- **Threshold (2026, must be verified):** sources conflict — a **1 bln UZS** annual-income threshold is cited for turnover tax (azma.uz), while the June 1, 2026 reform reportedly raised the general-regime/VAT transition threshold to **5 bln UZS** (spot.uz, gazeta.uz). **Confirm the currently effective threshold on soliq.uz.**
- **Date shifting:** if a deadline falls on a non-working day, it moves to the next working day.
