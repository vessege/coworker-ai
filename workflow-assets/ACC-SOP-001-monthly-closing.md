# 🇺🇿 Oylik buxgalteriya yopilishi va hisobot (SOP)

## Maqsad
Har oyni o'z vaqtida, xatosiz yopish va soliq hisobotlarini muddatida topshirish.

## Bosqichlar (oy oxiri → keyingi oy 20-sanasi)
1. **Birlamchi hujjatlarni yig'ish** — barcha kiruvchi/chiquvchi hisobvaraq-fakturalar, aktlar, bank ko'chirmalari.
2. **Solishtirish (sverka)** — kontragentlar bilan qarzdorlik, bank qoldig'i, kassa.
3. **Ish haqi va badallar** — ish haqini hisoblash, ijtimoiy soliq/badallarni tayyorlash.
4. **Soliqlarni hisoblash** — QQS, foyda solig'i (yoki aylanma soliq) summalarini hisoblash.
5. **Hisobotlarni shakllantirish** — shaxsiy kabinet (my.soliq.uz) da deklaratsiyalarni to'ldirish.
6. **Nazorat/tekshiruv** — summalar birlamchi hujjatlarga mos ekanini tekshirish.
7. **Topshirish va to'lash** — muddatlarga rioya (10 / 15 / 20-sanalar — ACC-KA-001).
8. **Arxivlash** — hujjatlar va hisobotlarni saqlash.

## Muddat nazorati (ACC-KA-001 bilan bog'liq)
- 10-sana: aksiz, mol-mulk/yer bo'nagi
- 15-sana: ijtimoiy badallar reyestri
- 20-sana: QQS + foyda solig'i hisoboti
- Chorak yakuni: aylanma soliq

## Xatolardan saqlanish
- Hisobotni birlamchi hujjatsiz shakllantirmang.
- Muddatni 5 ish kunidan oshirmang (jarima yengilligi sharti — ACC-KA-004).
- Har topshirishdan oldin summalarni solishtiring.

# 🇬🇧 Monthly Accounting Close & Reporting (SOP)

## Purpose
Close each month on time and error-free, and file tax reports by the deadlines.

## Steps (month-end → the 20th of next month)
1. **Collect primary documents** — all incoming/outgoing invoices, acts, bank statements.
2. **Reconcile** — counterparties, bank balance, cash.
3. **Payroll & contributions** — calculate wages, prepare social tax/contributions.
4. **Calculate taxes** — VAT, profit tax (or turnover tax) amounts.
5. **Prepare reports** — fill declarations in the personal cabinet (my.soliq.uz).
6. **Review** — verify amounts match the primary documents.
7. **File & pay** — meet deadlines (10th / 15th / 20th — ACC-KA-001).
8. **Archive** — store documents and reports.

## Deadline control (linked to ACC-KA-001)
- 10th: excise, property/land advance
- 15th: social contributions register
- 20th: VAT + profit tax reports
- Quarter-end: turnover tax

## Mistakes to avoid
- Do not prepare a report without primary documents.
- Never exceed 5 working days late (fine-relief condition — ACC-KA-004).
- Reconcile amounts before every submission.
