# 🇺🇿 Kiruvchi hujjatlarni ro'yxatga olish (SOP)

## Maqsad
Tashkilotga tashqaridan kelgan har bir hujjatni yo'qotmasdan qabul qilish,
ro'yxatga olish, tegishli ijrochiga yetkazish va ijro nazoratini ta'minlash.

## Qo'llanilishi
Ofis-menejer / kotib. Barcha kiruvchi hujjatlar: xatlar, arizalar, shikoyatlar,
shartnomalar, idoralararo yozishmalar (qog'oz va elektron).

## Rollar
- **Ofis-menejer** — qabul qiladi, ro'yxatga oladi, taqsimlaydi, nazorat qiladi.
- **Rahbar** — rezolyutsiya (ijrochi va muddatni) belgilaydi.
- **Ijrochi** — hujjatni ijro etadi.

## Bosqichlar
1. **Qabul qilish.** Hujjatni qabul qiling. Konvert/yetkazilishning butunligini
   tekshiring. Shaxsiy belgili ("shaxsan") hujjatlar ochilmaydi — adresatga uzatiladi.
2. **Dastlabki ko'rik.** Hujjat shu tashkilotga tegishli ekanini, barcha ilovalar
   borligini tekshiring. Adashib kelgan hujjat jo'natuvchiga qaytariladi.
3. **Ro'yxatga olish.** Kiruvchi hujjatlar jurnaliga (yoki EHAT tizimiga) yozing:
   ketma-ket **kiruvchi raqam**, qabul sanasi, jo'natuvchi, chiquvchi raqami/sanasi,
   qisqa mazmuni, ilovalar soni. Hujjatning birinchi varag'iga ro'yxat shtampi bosiladi.
4. **Rahbarga taqdim etish.** Ro'yxatga olingan hujjat rahbarga ko'rib chiqish uchun beriladi.
5. **Rezolyutsiyani qayd etish.** Rahbar belgilagan ijrochi va ijro muddatini
   jurnalga/tizimga kiriting.
6. **Ijrochiga topshirish.** Hujjatni ijrochiga imzo qo'ydirib (yoki tizim orqali) bering.
7. **Ijro nazorati.** Muddatni kuzatib boring. Muddatdan oldin eslatma yuboring.
8. **Ijroni yopish.** Ijro tugagach, "ijro etildi" belgisini qo'ying va hujjatni
   tegishli ishga (papka/nomenklatura) tikib qo'ying.

## Ijro muddatlari (standart)
- Muddat rezolyutsiyada ko'rsatilgan bo'lsa — o'sha muddat.
- Ko'rsatilmagan bo'lsa — odatda 10 ish kuni (ichki reglament bilan belgilanadi).
- Fuqarolar murojaatlari — qonun bilan belgilangan muddat (alohida SOP).

## Nazorat nuqtalari
- Har bir hujjat noyob kiruvchi raqamga ega.
- Ro'yxatga olinmagan hujjat ijrochiga berilmaydi.
- Muddati o'tgan hujjatlar bo'yicha kunlik hisobot.

## Xatolardan saqlanish
- Hujjatni ro'yxatga olishdan oldin ijrochiga bermang.
- Bir hujjatga ikkita raqam bermang.
- Elektron va qog'oz nusxa bir xil kiruvchi raqam ostida bog'lansin.

# 🇬🇧 Incoming Document Registration (SOP)

## Purpose
Ensure every document received from outside the organization is accepted,
registered, routed to the right assignee, and tracked to completion without loss.

## Scope
Office manager / secretary. All inbound documents: letters, applications,
complaints, contracts, inter-agency correspondence (paper and electronic).

## Roles
- **Office manager** — receives, registers, routes, monitors.
- **Manager/Director** — writes the resolution (assignee + deadline).
- **Assignee** — executes the document.

## Steps
1. **Receive.** Accept the document; check package integrity. Items marked
   "personal" are not opened — forwarded to the addressee.
2. **Initial review.** Confirm it belongs to this organization and all
   attachments are present. Misdelivered items are returned to sender.
3. **Register.** Record in the incoming register (or the electronic document
   management system): sequential **incoming number**, date received, sender,
   sender's outgoing number/date, short summary, attachment count. Stamp the
   registration mark on the first page.
4. **Present to manager.** Give the registered document to the manager for review.
5. **Record the resolution.** Enter the assignee and deadline set by the manager.
6. **Hand over to assignee.** Deliver against signature (or via the system).
7. **Execution control.** Track the deadline; send a reminder before it expires.
8. **Close execution.** Mark "executed" and file the document in the correct
   case folder (nomenclature of files).

## Standard deadlines
- Use the deadline in the resolution if given.
- If none — commonly 10 working days (set by internal regulation).
- Citizens' appeals — statutory deadline (separate SOP).

## Control points
- Every document has a unique incoming number.
- No unregistered document is handed to an assignee.
- Daily report of overdue documents.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Do not route before registering.
- Never assign two numbers to one document.
- Link the electronic and paper copies under the same incoming number.
